Round 2 · Palette exploration

Same design. Four refinements for premium feel.

You picked Direction A — warm editorial. This document explores four palette shifts on top of that same design system. Same layout, same typography, same product strategy. Only color and a few finishing details change. Scroll through each. Don't pick the one that's prettiest. Pick the one that's most "AajooHomes."

One thing to know before you scroll

Premium brands restrict color. Aman Resorts uses 2. Hermès uses 2. Loro Piana uses 1 + black. The instinct to "add more colors to look premium" is wrong — the move is to restrict harder. Every palette below removes more than it adds.

Onyx & Champagne

Near-black hero against cream page. Muted gold accent replaces terracotta. The "luxury hotel" move — Aman, Soho House, Six Senses live here. Furthest from Airbnb.

— My recommendation

If forced to pick one, Option 02.

The original Forest & Terracotta direction was already in the right zone — the refinements in Option 02 (Forest & Bone) push it where it needs to go without rebuilding what's working. Deeper green for confidence. Terracotta dialed back so it's a finishing touch, not a constant interruption. Same brand promise, more restraint.

If the client wants to be bolder, go Option 03 (Sand & Indigo). It's the most India-specific palette and the hardest for Airbnb to copy. Best long-term differentiation, slightly higher execution risk.

Avoid Option 01 (Onyx) unless the client commits to a luxury-only inventory strategy — it's wrong for ₹3K–8K listings. Avoid Option 04 (Charcoal Minimal) unless they're funding a photography refresh — bad photos kill it.
