About this home

Casa Branca is a 1907 Portuguese-Goan villa tucked behind a high white wall in Vagator's quietest lane. Step inside and the temperature drops six degrees — that's what 14-foot ceilings and original laterite walls do. The villa sleeps eight in four bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, mosquito-netted four-poster beds, and a private balcony overlooking either the pool or the garden.

The previous owners spent four years restoring the place before listing with us. Floors are original. Furniture is mostly antique. The kitchen has been brought up to modern standards but kept visually period.
